# Pomfret Astro Network

**Status:** Canonical product definition (2026-08-31)  
**Purpose:** Shared reference for product, About copy, outreach, and multi-site work. For implementation and operations, see the [repository README](../README.md).

## Definition

Pomfret Astro is a platform for automating, remotely operating, and connecting independent observatories.

It began at Pomfret School’s Olmsted Observatory with a simple problem: an observatory should not sit unused just because an experienced operator cannot be on site every night. Pomfret Astro brings observing plans, coordination among multiple users, automatic scheduling, weather-aware decisions, telescope control, live status, and data delivery into one system.

That idea now extends beyond a single observatory. The Pomfret Astro Network is intended to connect independently operated sites through a common platform. Those sites may belong to schools, individuals, universities, astronomy organizations, or remote observatory operators. Ownership does not determine whether an observatory can participate.

Every observatory retains its ownership and governance. Its operator remains in control of equipment, users, and access rules. Joining the Network does not mean handing over control or committing to share telescope time.

Pomfret Astro provides infrastructure for autonomous operation and coordination among users. A site can use that infrastructure while remaining entirely private. An operator who chooses to open some observing capacity can also make connections with people and other observatories through the Network.

The goal is to help independent observatories make better use of the equipment they already have and, when they choose, connect them into a broader observing community. The Network is a way to link observatories without erasing their independence.
